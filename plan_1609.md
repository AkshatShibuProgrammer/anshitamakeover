## 2. Admin Multi-Addon & Vendor Commission Architecture

Since pricing for Photography, Event Management, Catering, etc. is dynamic, we give **full control to the Admin** with AI-assisted bundle creation and commission margin calculation:

### A. Supported Addon Categories
Admin can create custom bundles with any vendor category:
1. 📸 **Photography & Videography** (Candid, Traditional, Cinematic Reels, Drone)
2. 🎪 **Event Planning & Decor** (Mandap, Stage, Entrance, Haldi setup)
3. 🍽️ **Catering & Hospitality** (Per-plate vendor packages)
4. 💆 **Salon & Pre-Bridal Care** (Facial, Hair Spa, Body Polishing)
5. 🎶 **DJ, Sound & Entertainment** (Sangeet anchor, sound setup)

### B. Admin Dynamic Controls
For each partner/addon service, Admin can configure:
- **Base Vendor Cost**: What the external vendor charges Anshita (e.g. ₹50,000 for Photography).
- **Client Quote Price**: What client sees in the bundle (e.g. ₹65,000).
- **Studio Commission / Margin (%)**: Automatically calculated ROI (e.g. ₹15,000 / 23% profit margin).
- **Package Status**: "Instant Estimate" or "On Request / Custom Quote".

### C. AI-Assisted Bundle Suggester & Optimizer
The Admin AI Copilot can:
- **Suggest Profitable Combos**: e.g., *"If client books Bridal Makeup + Photography, offer a 10% bundle concession while preserving ₹18,000 commission for the studio."*
- **Auto-Generate Custom Quote on WhatsApp**: Generates a professional branded quotation breakdown for the client including makeup + addons.
